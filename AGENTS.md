# Topical Knowledge Cell Brokerage Service

This settlement qualifies a finite customer-supplied denominator of
provider-authored topical knowledge-cell product listings. It does not crawl,
publish, rank, award, buy, install, or operate any product. Qualified, refused,
and unknown are distinct outcomes; unknown is never false or zero.

Package, Agent Card, RMN identity, and RWIL/RDF evidence are obtained from
separately hired providers. Comparison is componentwise and preserves typed
prices without exchange-rate invention. Human customers retain selection,
usefulness, admission, spend, and publication decisions.
